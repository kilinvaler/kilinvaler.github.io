How much water do you need

“Drink two litres a day” is too general. A person who weighs 50 kg and a person who weighs 90 kg need different amounts of water.

Water Wisdom calculates your goal from your weight: about 30 ml per kilogram for women and 31 ml for men. Then the goal adjusts to your life:

  • Hot climate — more; cool climate — a little less.
  • A workout in “My routine” adds water to that day’s goal, depending on how long it lasts.
  • Lots of steps during the day — a little more water too.

Tea, coffee and juice count as well, with an adjustment: water counts in full, other drinks partly.

You can also set your goal by hand if your doctor has recommended a different amount.
