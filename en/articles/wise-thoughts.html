Why read wise thoughts every day

A short thought takes less than a minute to read. But when it comes at the right moment, it can change your mood for the whole day. And if you remember it and apply it in the right situation, it brings real practical benefit: it shows you how to act, how to hold back or where to start.

The beauty of wise sayings is that a few words hold someone’s long experience. “Water wears away stone not by force but by persistence” — that is about water, and about any good habit.

That is why in Water Wisdom every glass comes with a thought. Drinking water is a habit for the body; pausing for a second to think is a habit for the mind.
