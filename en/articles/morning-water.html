A glass of water in the morning

During the night we go seven or eight hours without drinking, yet we keep breathing and sweating a little the whole time. That is why the body is especially short of water in the morning, even if you are not thirsty yet.

A glass of water right after waking up is the simplest habit to start the day with. It does not replace breakfast and it works no miracles, but it helps you wake up — and gives you the first 250 ml towards your daily goal.

How to make it a habit

  • Put a glass or a bottle of water by your bed in the evening.
  • Drink calmly, in small sips, at room temperature.
  • Log the glass in the app: the first step of the day is done.

This is general information, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, ask your doctor how much you should drink.
